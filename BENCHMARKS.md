## How to reproduce

```bash
./.venv/bin/python auto_optimize.py \
    --model <MODEL_KEY> --judge --results results.json
./.venv/bin/python generate_report.py \
    --input results.json --output leaderboard.md
```

`auto_optimize.py` searches the highest loadable GPU offload ratio (SDK transport only) and the largest context up to `--max_context` (default 16384). With `--judge`, each trial's output is scored 1-10 by an LLM-as-a-judge; when a reasoning model spends the whole token budget thinking (empty visible output), the judge step is skipped rather than recording a misleading 1/10.
